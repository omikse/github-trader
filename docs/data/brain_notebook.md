## Lessons
- Stops within ~1 h1 ATR die in chop. Use 4h structure, size down.
- Stop beyond 4h high + buffer, not at it. Low-liquidity alts (DOT) can spike 10% on a wick: a tight stop there is a coin flip; size smaller or use wider stops.
- Middle-of-range bounce shorts in chop get wicked. Prefer range-edge or breakout entries.
- Trailing behind completed 4h highs/lows worked (BTC +4.98, AVAX +2.20, PEPE +5.12). Trailing to ~entry gives it back.
- BE stops in quiet hours get gapped out; cost entries.
- Breakout orders right at the 24h high fill on a wick and reverse. Put trigger with buffer above.
- Failed-breakout fade on memes with crowded funding works; don't short memes when funding is deeply negative.
- Stacking same-direction bets = one bet; count combined risk.
- Meme-coin carry: extreme funding flips within hours (BONK +185% -> -106% in a day).
- Pre-event breakout longs (BTC/XRP 27-28 Oct) got stopped as the move faded hours before Fed: don't chase pre-event breakouts; wait for the event reaction.
