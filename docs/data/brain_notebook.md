## Lessons
- Stops within ~1 h1 ATR die in chop. Use 4h structure, size down.
- Stop must sit beyond the 4h high + buffer (~0.5-1 ATR), not at it (DOT 1.033 wick lesson).
- Middle-of-range bounce shorts in chop get wicked. Prefer range-edge or breakout entries.
- Trailing behind completed 4h highs worked (BTC +4.98, AVAX +2.20, PEPE +5.12). Trailing to ~entry gives it back.
- Breakout orders right at the 24h high can fill on a wick and reverse (BTC 81,150 filled at 81,174 top). Prefer trigger after a 4h close or a bit higher buffer.
- Failed-breakout fade on memes with crowded funding works; don't short memes when funding is deeply negative.
- Stacking same-direction bets = one bet; count combined risk.
- Meme-coin carry: extreme funding flips within hours; fees ate BONK carry.
- Git: clone may be on detached HEAD; fetch, checkout --detach origin/main, commit, push HEAD:main.
