## Lessons
- Stops within ~1 h1 ATR die in chop. Use 4h structure, size down.
- Stop must sit beyond the 4h high + buffer (~0.5-1 ATR), not at it (DOT 1.033 wick lesson).
- Middle-of-range bounce shorts in chop get wicked. Prefer range-edge or breakout entries.
- Trailing behind completed 4h highs worked (BTC +4.98, AVAX +2.20, PEPE +5.12). But trailing to ~entry gives it back: ETH short trailed to 2331 (entry 2333) closed ~flat (-0.13) on pre-Fed bounce; SUI similar risk. Lock gains a bit further from entry or accept the flat exit.
- Failed-breakout fade on memes with crowded funding works; don't short memes when funding is deeply negative.
- Stacking same-direction alt shorts = one bet; count combined risk.
- Meme-coin carry: extreme funding flips within hours; fees ate BONK carry.
- Re-derive trail levels from current 4h highs, never place a stop at/under mark.
