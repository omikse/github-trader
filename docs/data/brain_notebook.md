## Lessons
- Stops within ~1 h1 ATR die in chop. Use 4h structure, size down.
- DOT short stop 1.033 sat right at the 4h high (1.0332/1.0336): wick took it by 0.0005 then price fell 3%. Stop must sit beyond the high + buffer (~0.5-1 ATR), not at it.
- Middle-of-range bounce shorts in weekend chop get stopped by wicks. Prefer range-edge or breakout entries.
- Trailing behind completed 4h highs worked: BTC +4.98, AVAX +2.20, PEPE short +5.12, ADA short +0.19. SUI trail to near-entry turned +3 into -0.06: lock gains a bit further from entry.
- Failed-breakout fade on memes with crowded funding works; don't short memes when funding is deeply negative.
- Stacking same-direction alt shorts = one bet; count combined risk.
- BE stop exactly at entry = noise exit; give a buffer.
- Meme-coin carry: extreme funding flips within hours; fees ate BONK carry.
- Trail levels in notebook go stale: re-derive from current 4h highs, never place a stop at/under mark.
